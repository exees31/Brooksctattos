Tattoo Fun Facts

  • The word "tattoo" comes from the Polynesian word "tatau," meaning to strike or tap.
  • In the 1800s, tattoos were a sign of status and wealth among European royals.
  • The oldest evidence of tattoos dates back to Ötzi the Iceman, who lived around 3300 B.C.
